Getting there

Pier 3, The Embarcadero.

Boarding is at Pier 3 on The Embarcadero in San Francisco. Pier 3 is easy to reach by Ferry, Muni, and BART (Embarcadero station). We strongly suggest arriving very early, ferry or ride share is recommended. Public parking is available at Pier 3 for $20 if you drive.
